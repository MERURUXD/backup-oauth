Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-13

sukimi-rclone-backup is used only by its owner. Using the Google Drive drive.file scope, it creates, reads, and deletes only the encrypted backup files it uploads to the owner's own Google Drive. It does not access any other files.

No data is collected, sold, or shared with any third party. Backup files are encrypted before upload. OAuth tokens are stored only on the owner's own server.

Access can be revoked at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.
